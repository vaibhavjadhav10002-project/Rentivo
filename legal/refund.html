Refund Policy

Last updated: [Insert date at launch]

This is a standard-form refund policy template. It should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel for your jurisdiction, and updated to match your actual billing provider's terms, before public launch.

1. Free plan

Rentivo's Starter plan is free and carries no billing, so no refund applies.

2. Paid subscriptions

Professional and Enterprise plans are billed monthly or yearly, in advance. If you cancel, you'll continue to have access until the end of your current billing period — we don't pro-rate mid-cycle cancellations.

3. Requesting a refund

If you believe you were billed in error, or experienced a service issue that materially prevented you from using Rentivo, contact us within 14 days of the charge and we'll review your request in good faith.

4. Non-refundable circumstances

  • Change of mind after a billing period has already started and been substantially used.
  • Account suspension due to a violation of our Terms & Conditions.

5. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

6. Contact us

Refund requests can be sent via our Contact page.
