This is a standard-form privacy policy template for Rentivo. It should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel for your jurisdiction before public launch.

1. Information we collect

When you use Rentivo as a property owner or tenant, we collect:

  • Account information: name, email, phone number, and password (stored securely, never in plain text).
  • Property and tenancy data: property details, room/bed assignments, rent amounts, agreements, and payment records.
  • Identity verification data: Aadhaar number and document images, submitted voluntarily during tenant onboarding for owner verification purposes.
  • Usage data: device type, app version, and general interaction logs, used to improve reliability.
  • Push notification tokens, if you enable notifications.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to operate the core service — managing properties, tenants, rent collection, agreements, and notifications — and to communicate service-related updates to you. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data sharing

Property owners and their tenants share certain data with each other by design (e.g. a tenant's profile is visible to their property's owner). We use trusted infrastructure providers (such as our database and hosting providers) solely to operate the service, under contracts that require them to protect your data.

4. Data security

Access to tenant and property data is restricted at the database level — an owner account can only ever access data belonging to their own properties, enforced by row-level security policies, not just application logic.

5. Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, correction of inaccurate data, or deletion of your account and associated data at any time. See our Data Deletion Policy for details.

6. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll notify you of material changes through the app or by email.

8. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@rentivo.example.com or via our Contact page.
