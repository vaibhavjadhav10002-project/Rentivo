Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: [Insert date at launch]

This is a standard-form data deletion policy template. It should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel for your jurisdiction before public launch, and the request channel below should be connected to a real process.

1. Your right to delete your data

You can request permanent deletion of your Rentivo account and associated personal data at any time, whether you're a property owner or a tenant.

2. How to request deletion

  1. Email privacy@rentivo.example.com from the email address on your account, with the subject line "Data Deletion Request," or use our Contact page.
  2. We'll verify your identity to protect against fraudulent deletion requests.
  3. We'll confirm once your data has been deleted, typically within 30 days.

3. What gets deleted

Your account profile, uploaded documents, and personal identifiers are permanently deleted. Property owners: note that deleting your account will affect any tenants linked to your properties — we'll work with you on the right sequence if you manage active tenancies.

4. What we may retain

We may retain certain records where required by law (for example, financial transaction records for tax/audit purposes) for the minimum period required, even after account deletion.

5. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent via our Contact page.
