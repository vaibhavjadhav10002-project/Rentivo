FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Rentivo free to use? +

Yes — the Starter plan is free for a single property with up to 15 tenants. Paid plans unlock unlimited properties and advanced features.

Do I need to install the Android app, or can I just use the browser? +

Either works. Rentivo runs as a full installable PWA in any modern browser, or as a native Android app with the same features and push notifications.

How do tenants get added to my property? +

You send an invite from the Owner Dashboard. The tenant sets a password, completes their own profile, and you approve it — no manual data entry on your end.

How does rent collection work? +

Tenants pay via QR code or your preferred UPI method, and payments are recorded with a reference number against their ledger automatically.

Is my data secure? +

Every property's data is isolated at the database level — an owner can only ever see their own properties and tenants, enforced by row-level security, not just app-level checks.

Can I manage more than one PG or property? +

Yes — Rentivo is built multi-property from the ground up. Switch between properties from a single account.

What happens if I lose internet connection? +

Core actions like visitor and parcel logging keep working offline and sync automatically once you're back online. Other actions will let you know if a connection is needed.

How do I get support? +

Visit the Help Center or reach us directly from the Contact page.
