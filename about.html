About Rentivo

The operating system for property management

Rentivo started from a simple observation: most PG and property owners in India still run their business on a mix of notebooks, WhatsApp groups, and memory. Rent gets tracked by hand. Tenant details live in someone's head. A single missed reminder means a missed payment.

We built Rentivo to be the single place an owner actually needs — properties, rooms, tenants, payments, agreements, and reports, all connected, all in one dashboard, with a tenant-facing app that means owners stop being the middleman for every small update.

We're early. We're building this alongside real property owners, and we'd rather ship something genuinely useful for a handful of PGs than something generic for thousands. If that sounds like your business, we'd like to hear from you.
